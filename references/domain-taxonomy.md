# Domain Taxonomy

25 domains covering the vast majority of refined-prompt requests. Each entry: signal keywords (TR + EN), role phrasing template, conflict resolution notes.

When multiple domains match, use **the dominant signal rule**: pick the domain whose keywords appear earliest and most frequently. List secondary domains as "supporting" only if they materially shape deliverable structure.

---

## Strategy & Product

### 1. Product Growth Strategy
- **TR signals:** onboarding, aktivasyon, retention, kullanıcı yolculuğu, funnel, dönüşüm, ürün stratejisi, growth, churn azaltma
- **EN signals:** onboarding, activation, retention, user journey, funnel, conversion, product strategy, growth, churn
- **Role:** "Senior Product Growth Strategist with N years of B2C/B2B SaaS experience"
- **Conflict resolution:** If "design" or "screens" also present → secondary UX Design.

### 2. UX Strategy / UX Design
- **TR signals:** kullanıcı deneyimi, arayüz, wireframe, prototip, kullanılabilirlik, ekran tasarımı, UX, UI
- **EN signals:** user experience, interface, wireframe, prototype, usability, screen design, UX, UI
- **Role:** "Lead UX Strategist with deep expertise in [B2B/consumer/enterprise] product design"

### 3. Product Management
- **TR signals:** roadmap, ürün gereksinimleri, PRD, özellik önceliklendirme, sprint planı, kullanıcı hikayeleri
- **EN signals:** roadmap, product requirements, PRD, feature prioritization, sprint plan, user stories
- **Role:** "Senior Product Manager with experience shipping [B2B SaaS / consumer / platform] products"

### 4. Business Strategy
- **TR signals:** iş modeli, rekabet analizi, pazar girişi, GTM, stratejik plan, SWOT
- **EN signals:** business model, competitive analysis, market entry, GTM, strategic plan, SWOT
- **Role:** "Senior Strategy Consultant with experience at top-tier consulting firms"

## Conflict Resolution Cheat Sheet

| User signal mix | Dominant | Supporting |
|---|---|---|
| "API tasarla + güvenlik gereksinimleri" | Backend Engineering | Security Engineering |
| "Onboarding stratejisi + ekran tasarımı" | Product Growth | UX Design |
| "İş ilanı yaz + JD" | Talent Acquisition | Copywriting |
| "Finansal model + büyüme stratejisi" | Financial Modeling | Business Strategy |
| "Marketing planı + içerik takvimi" | Content Marketing | (alone) |
| "GDPR uyum sözleşmesi" | Legal | Compliance |
| "Veri pipeline + ML feature store" | Data Engineering | ML Engineering |
| Vague / single short phrase | Research / Analysis (fallback) | — |

When in doubt: pick the domain that determines the **deliverable's structural shape**. A "marketing plan with finance projections" is Marketing because the deliverable is a marketing plan; the projections are an embedded section.
